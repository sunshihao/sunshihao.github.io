## Adjustments to the Taiwan storefront of the App Store on iOS and iPadOS

October 29, 2026

Apple is making changes to the Taiwan storefront of the App Store, following discussions with the Taiwan Fair Trade Commission.

As of October 31, 2026, the commission rate for standard Apple In-App Purchase and paid app transactions will be 26% on the Taiwan storefront of the App Store on iOS and iPadOS. Currently, that rate is 30%.

Signing the updated terms by October 31 is not required to receive the benefit of these commission rate changes starting that date.

The [Apple Developer Program License Agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/) has been revised to support updated policies. You can [sign in to your account](https://developer.apple.com/account/) to accept the updated terms.
