# OPEC+ maintains crude oil production targets for December 2026

[*arrow\_upward*](#top "Go to top")

[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/intent/compose?text=OPEC+ maintains crude oil production targets for December 2026%20https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/opec-maintains-crude-oil-production-targets-december-2026.html) [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/sharing/share-offsite/?url=https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/opec-maintains-crude-oil-production-targets-december-2026.html) [Facebook](http://www.facebook.com/sharer.php?u=https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/opec-maintains-crude-oil-production-targets-december-2026.html&p=OPEC+ maintains crude oil production targets for December 2026)

**The seven OPEC+ members** (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman) have agreed to **maintain their crude oil production targets at current levels for December 2026**, following a virtual meeting held on 1 November 2026 (OPEC+ press release, 01/11/2026).

The **production quotas** remain unchanged at **10.5 mb/d** for Saudi Arabia, **9.9 mb/d** for Russia, **4.4 mb/d** for Iraq, **2.7 mb/d** for Kuwait, **1.6 mb/d** for Kazakhstan, **1 mb/d** for Algeria and **0.8 mb/d** for Oman. This corresponds to **the production levels set for September 2026**, which were also maintained in October and November (KEI, 06/10/2026).

Although Gulf members have been increasing exports, which had been disrupted for several months by the conflict in the Middle East, most are still producing **below their allocated targets**.
