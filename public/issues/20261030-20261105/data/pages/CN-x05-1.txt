# Chinese shares close lower Friday

Source: Xinhua

Editor: huaxia

2026-10-30 16:51:15

BEIJING, Oct. 30 (Xinhua) -- Chinese stocks closed lower Friday, with the benchmark Shanghai Composite Index down 1.44 percent to 3,599.02 points.

The Shenzhen Component Index closed 2.73 percent lower at 10,833.78 points.

Turnover of stocks covered by the two indices totaled about 1.69 trillion yuan (around 252 billion U.S. dollars), up from 1.56 trillion yuan on the previous trading day.

Shipping, banking, food and beverage stocks were among the gainers, while shares in media and communications declined sharply.

The ChiNext Index, tracking China's Nasdaq-style board of growth enterprises, lost 4.13 percent to close at 2,413.63 points Friday.

The STAR Composite Index, which reflects the performance of stocks on China's sci-tech innovation board, closed 4.66 percent lower at 1,421.31 points.  ■
