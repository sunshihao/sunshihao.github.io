# Crude futures settle lower

Source: Xinhua

Editor: huaxia

2026-11-03 04:17:00

![](zxcode_202611032976a05ed1b04ff7a948e30e83202611.jpg)

NEW YORK, Nov. 2 (Xinhua) -- Oil prices fell on Monday.

The West Texas Intermediate for December delivery dropped 7.66 dollars, or 7.36 percent, to settle at 96.46 U.S. dollars a barrel on the New York Mercantile Exchange. Brent crude for January delivery decreased by 8.66 dollars, or 7.63 percent, to settle at 104.77 dollars a barrel on the London ICE Futures Exchange. ■
