Weekly Petroleum Status Report/Energy Information Administration
v
For the week ending September 11, 2026, U.S. refineries processed 17.3 
million barrels per day (b/d), down 256,000 b/d from the previous week, 
at 96.8% capacity utilization. Gasoline output averaged 9.6 million b/d, and 
distillate production decreased to 5.2 million b/d. 
Crude oil imports increased 234,000 b/d to 7.1 million b/d, and the four-
week average of 6.7 million b/d is 7.5% above the year-ago level. Gasoline 
imports averaged 537,000 b/d; distillate imports averaged 114,000 b/d. 
Commercial crude oil inventories (excluding the Strategic Petroleum 
Reserve) decreased 0.6 million barrels to 423.4 million barrels, 1% above 
the five-year average. Gasoline inventories increased 0.8 million barrels, 
5% below the five-year average. Distillate inventories increased 1.6 million 
barrels, 13% below the five-year average. Propane/propylene inventories 
decreased 1.4 million barrels, 22% above the five-year average. Total 
commercial petroleum inventories increased by 2.6 million barrels for the 
week.
Over the past four weeks, total product supplied averaged 20.5 million 
b/d, down 0.6% year over year. The four-week average for gasoline product 
supplied decreased 1.0% year over year to 8.8 million b/d, while the four-
week average for distillate product supplied decreased 3.3% to 3.6 million 
b/d. The four-week average for jet fuel product supplied increased 4.4% year 
over year.
The price for West Texas Intermediate crude oil was $101.27 per barrel on 
September 11, 2026, $8.58 more than a week ago, and $38.25 more than a 
year ago. The New Y ork Harbor spot price for conventional gasoline was 
$3.376 per gallon, $0.131 more than a week ago, and $1.380 more than the 
year-ago price. The spot price for No. 2 heating oil at New Y ork Harbor was 
$4.910 per gallon, $0.456 more than a week ago, and $2.714 more than the 
price last year.
The national average retail price for regular gasoline increased to $4.319 per 
gallon on September 14, 2026, $0.162 more than last week’s price, and $1.151 
more than the year-ago price. The national average retail diesel fuel price 
increased $0.318 to $6.285 per gallon, $2.546 more than the price one year 
ago.
