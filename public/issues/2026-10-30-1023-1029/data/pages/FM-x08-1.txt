## Thrive Holdings will uphold ADT’s longstanding commitment to protecting customer data, and ADT’s data protection and privacy policies will remain unchanged.
ADT and Thrive Holdings, a permanent capital holding company focused on investing in established businesses and advancing their capabilities through technology, today announced a definitive agreement under which Thrive Holdings will acquire ADT in an all-cash transaction.
The agreement values ADT at approximately $7.6 billion in equity value and $15.3 billion in enterprise value.
Under the terms of the agreement, which ADT’s Board of Directors unanimously approved, ADT stockholders will receive $10.00 per share in cash upon completion. The per share price represents a premium of 45.6% to ADT’s unaffected closing share price on Oct.
27, 2026, and 52.1% to its 30-day volume-weighted average share price ending on Oct. 27, 2026.
The transaction would combine ADT’s more than 150 years of security experience, nationwide service network and customer trust with Thrive Holdings’ long-term capital and engineering capabilities. Thrive Holdings is a permanent capital holding company, which enables it to own and operate businesses over a long-term horizon.
This ownership structure will provide ADT with greater flexibility to invest in its people and the development of new products and services.
“ADT is pleased to enter into this transaction, which recognizes the value built by our more than 12,000 employees and delivers an attractive cash premium to our stockholders,” said Jim DeVries, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer of ADT.

With Thrive Holdings’ long-term capital and technology expertise, ADT will gain new opportunities to accelerate innovation, enhance the customer experience and bring greater peace of mind to even more homes and businesses.”
“ADT has spent generations showing up for people when it matters most,” said Kareem Zaki, Partner at Thrive Holdings. “We intend to build on that foundation by strengthening ADT’s technology and product capabilities, with the capital and patience to invest over decades.

...

Currently, more than 75 businesses across accounting, IT services and the built environment are part of Thrive Holdings. Following the closing of the transaction, ADT will form the foundation of Thrive Holdings’ smart home security business.

## Transaction Terms and Timing
Financing commitments for the transaction have been solely provided by funds managed by affiliates of Apollo Global Management, Inc.
The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions, including approval by ADT’s stockholders and receipt of U.S. antitrust approval. The transaction is not subject to a financing condition.
Under the terms of the merger agreement, ADT, with the assistance of its financial advisors, may solicit proposals from third parties for a period of 30 days continuing until 11:59 p.m., New York City time, November 28, 2026, with a potential 10-day extension for certain parties that submit acquisition proposals during the initial go-shop period.
There can be no assurance that the solicitation process will result in a superior proposal, and ADT does not intend to disclose developments with respect to the go-shop process unless and until it determines such disclosure is appropriate or otherwise required. ADT will have the right to terminate the merger agreement to enter into a superior proposal, subject to the terms and conditions of the merger agreement.
Until the transaction closes, ADT will continue to operate independently. Following completion, ADT will become privately held and its common stock will cease trading on the New York Stock Exchange.

## Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
As previously announced, ADT’s third quarter 2026 results will be issued on Nov. 3, 2026, prior to market opening. The company will share a summary of its results on a 10 a.m. ET conference call. In consideration of the announced transaction, the company will not be taking questions on the call.
