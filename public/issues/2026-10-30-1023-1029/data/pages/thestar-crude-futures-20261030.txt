# Crude futures settle lower
By [Kuai Bixiaoxin](https://www.thestar.com.my/authors?q=Kuai+Bixiaoxin)
Friday, 30 Oct 2026
| 3:14 AM MYT
share bookmark
NEW YORK, Oct. 29 (Xinhua) -- Oil prices fell on Thursday.
The West Texas Intermediate for December delivery lost 78 cents, or 0.76 percent, to settle at 102.00 U.S. dollars a barrel on the New York Mercantile Exchange. Brent crude for December delivery decreased by 44 cents, or 0.38 percent, to settle at 114.88 dollars a barrel on the London ICE Futures Exchange.
