# Democrats are ready to run Congress — and investigate all things Trump

The party will face a challenge in messaging how an aggressive oversight agenda will make life better for Americans.

![Image 1: Rep. Robert Garcia (D-Calif.) discusses what he called the &quot;DOGE DEMOLITION PLAN&quot; while Rep. Democrats are on track to win complete control of Congress, setting the stage for an unrelenting oversight platform in the next Congress that will zero in on both the Trump administration and the special interests that Republicans were reluctant to scrutinize over the last two years.

According to interviews with more than a dozen lawmakers, aides and attorneys, Democrats plan to use their new subpoena power and agenda-setting authority to target possible corruption among members of President Donald Trump’s family, [Elon Musk’s leadership](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/20/elon-musk-democrats-oversight-01127543) of the Department of Government Efficiency and the Department of Homeland Security’s aggressive immigration enforcement tactics.

They could demand testimony from the chief executives of Big Tech on social media addiction, the dangers of artificial intelligence and the negative impacts data centers are having on local communities. They could haul in the heads of leading oil companies to answer for allegations the industry waged a decadeslong climate misinformation campaign to continue reaping massive profits.

And these are just a few items on the to-do list.
