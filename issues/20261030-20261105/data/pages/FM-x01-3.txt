### What Happened in Markets Today

Stocks rallied following the midterm elections. Investors traditionally like divided government, and that could be all the more true under this president, who has given them headaches on everything from tariffs to Iran. The Dow Jones Industrial Average jumped 1,569 points, or 3.0%. The S&P 500 rose 1.6% and the Nasdaq gained 1.4%.
