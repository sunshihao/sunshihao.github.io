Crude oil prices fell more than 4% on Wednesday after Vice President JD Vance blamed the war against Iran for Republican midterm election reversals.

Brent crude futures, the international benchmark, were down 4.4% to $99.06 a barrel. U.S. West Texas Intermediate was 4.2% lower at $92.07.

"The reason we lost is the war," Vance said in a social media post Wednesday. "The president himself said so last night."

Democrats swept the House and Senate in Tuesday's elections, giving the party leverage to pressure President Donald Trump to end the unpopular war that has spiked gasoline and diesel prices.   
Trump told the Financial Times on Tuesday that he has no immediate plans to strike Iran. The oil market had priced in the risk of a major escalation after the elections, gaining more than 10% in October.

But oil futures have sold off this week as senior U.S. and Iranian officials indicated they're open to a diplomatic settlement to the conflict, which has disrupted global energy supplies for eight months.

"If you'd like to argue it was worth it, that losing the Senate for the next 6 years was worth it, then do so," Vance wrote. "Argue it was a worthwhile cost to pay. Agree or disagree, that's at least honest.

"But everyone knows why we lost," the vice president said. "And any honest conversation about where we go from here must start there.
