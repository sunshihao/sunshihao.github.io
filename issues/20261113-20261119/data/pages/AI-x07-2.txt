# Anthropic closes first day of public trading at $1.3tn valuation after largest-ever IPO

AI startup and Claude chatbot maker became one of the world’s most valuable publicly traded businesses with final share price of $97

Johana Bhuiyan

Thu 19 Nov 2026 17.09 EST

First published on Thu 19 Nov 2026 08.00 EST

Anthropic closed its first day of trading on the US stock market about 17% higher than its initial public offering price. At the close of market on Thursday, Anthropic shares were trading at just over $97 with a nearly $1.3tn total market capitalization, $1.75tn fully diluted.

While the final share value was higher than the IPO price of $83, it marked a dip from an early high of $111.50. The firm’s shares initially soared 34% when trading got under way on its US market debut on Thursday, valuing the company at more than $2tn. Nonetheless, Anthropic’s ranked among the biggest stock market flotations in history. The artificial intelligence startup, founded in 2021, became one of the most valuable publicly traded businesses in the world.

“Today puts the company in a stronger financial position, and in doing so only strengthens our resolve to do the right thing,” said Anthropic’s chief executive, Dario Amodei, who rang the Nasdaq opening bell.

Anthropic announced late on Wednesday that it had sold 980m shares to underwriters, an upsized offering that raised $81.3bn, surpassing the previous record set by SpaceX’s initial $75bn fundraise in June.

US stocks slumped at the end of trading, a potential sign that Anthropic’s mammoth IPO wasn’t enough to alleviate concerns over the AI industry’s volatility. Most of the major chipmakers’ stock prices closed the day lower than they started. While Nvidia hit its sixth day of losses after its latest earnings call, other players like Micron and Broadcom also saw their share prices decline.

The company’s listing serves as a bellwether for the multitrillion-dollar artificial intelligence boom as the first frontier lab to be publicly traded. Wall Street investors and the entire global tech industry are watching closely how it fares on the public market.

Anthropic’s stock market debut makes it the first firm focused entirely on AI “uncluttered by an ad business or a rocket business” to be publicly traded, analysts at Yorkville Ives said.

“Anthropic’s IPO is a big near-term win for the tech sector and should help restore some investor confidence that the AI Revolution growth thesis is not waning,” Yorkville Ives analysts wrote in a memo.
