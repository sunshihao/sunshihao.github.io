# Dow closes below 50,000; Nasdaq falls 2% as Anthropic IPO fails to resuscitate AI trade

Stocks tumbled on Thursday, falling to their lows of the session the very moment the widely anticipated artificial intelligence IPO Anthropic began trading.

The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite Add To Watchlist dropped 2.25% to end at 23,879.20 after gaining as much as 2% earlier in the session. The S&P 500 Add To Watchlist shed 2.02% and closed at 7,175.83. The Dow Jones Industrial Average Add To Watchlist lost 1,101.11 points, or 2.19%, to close at 49,097.06. The last time the 30-stock index closed below 50,000 was in June. At one point, the blue-chip average was down 10% from its intraday high.

Anthropic Add To Watchlist, trading at the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, opened shortly before noon at $111.50 per share, a 34% pop. The stock ended the day about 13% off its first trading price and closed up 17%.

The major averages fell to session lows immediately following the start of trading for the IPO, as investors appeared to take profits at the culmination of this much-hyped moment for the AI trade.

The AI company priced its initial public offering at $83 a share, above the expected range. That had chip stocks and other AI plays jumping in morning trading.

Chipmakers Nvidia Add To Watchlist and Micron Technology Add To Watchlist gave up their gains, along with tech giants Microsoft Add To Watchlist and Meta Platforms Add To Watchlist. Advanced Micro Devices Add To Watchlist lost nearly 8% after Hunterbrook Capital disclosed a short position in the name, pointing to alleged U.S. export control breaches that involve China.

"This was always going to be a critical test for the tech sector. Anthropic could be the new poster child for AI trade, especially after Nvidia headlines," said Aleks Spencer, Bogart Wealth CIO. "There's a lot here too that needs to be processed and digested, which translates to this period of uncertainty and volatility."

The Cboe Volatility Index (VIX) Add To Watchlist, or Wall Street's so-called fear gauge, reached a day high of close to 29 — its highest level since March — shortly after Anthropic began trading and investors moved out of tech stocks. The index was last above 28. 

"The enthusiasm comes in, and it's a great moment to sell the rally and take some profits," Spencer added.

The market has become more cautious on AI as of late, with investors focusing on whether heavy spending on the buildout will translate into meaningful profits. The iShares AI Innovation and Tech Active ETF (BAI) Add To Watchlist has fallen almost 14% this month, and embattled AI bellwether Nvidia has dropped 28% while the company grapples with the resignation of its CEO Jensen Huang.
