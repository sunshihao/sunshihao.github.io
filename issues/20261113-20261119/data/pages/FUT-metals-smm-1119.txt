# Metals fell almost across the board, LME tin and SHFE copper plunged, gold and silver continued to dive, crude oil closed lower [Overnight Market]

Published: Nov 19, 2026 08:27 (GMT+8)

SMM, November 19:

In the metals market:

Overnight, base metals on both ex-China and China markets fell broadly, with only LME lead and SHFE lead posting the smallest declines. LME lead fell 0.16%, and SHFE lead fell 0.22%. LME tin led the decline with a drop of 5.22%, while LME copper and SHFE copper fell more than 4%, with LME copper down 4.54% and SHFE copper down 4.96%. LME aluminum fell 3.37%, and SHFE aluminum fell 1.98%. LME zinc fell 3.96%, SHFE zinc fell 3.3%, LME nickel fell 3.72%, and SHFE nickel fell 3.12%. Alumina main contract rose 0.15%, while cast aluminum main contract fell 1.73%.

Overnight, ferrous metals showed mixed performance, with stainless steel down 1.98% and rebar up 0.57%. Iron ore rose 0.47%. In the coking coal and coke sector, coking coal fell 1.78%, and coke fell 0.89%.

Overnight in precious metals, COMEX gold fell 3.53%, hitting an intraday low of $3,721.8/oz, while COMEX silver fell 5.03%, dropping to an intraday low of $44.655/oz. In China, SHFE gold fell 2.4%, approaching the key level of 800 yuan/g, and SHFE silver fell 4.23%.
