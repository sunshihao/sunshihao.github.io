# Nvidia reverses, metals crash ahead of Anthropic IPO

Published Wed, Nov 18 2026 1:57 PM EST Updated Wed, Nov 18 2026 9:24 PM EST

Oliver Renick@OJRenick

Is this what it takes to clear room for $1.5 trillion of new supply?

Slumping crypto prices, a big Nvidia Add To Watchlist reversal despite good earnings, and now a sharp drop-off in metals prices from gold to copper — cross-asset selling has traders wondering if Anthropic's incoming IPO is spurring a rotation trade with the aim of freeing up cash to buy stock in the AI giant when it goes public on Thursday.

Shares of Nvidia swung from $164 overnight to below $152 during Wednesday's trading session despite smashing analysts' expectations on revenue and guidance. Options volume on the stock was almost three times the average and showed a bullish bias despite the reversal, with almost twice as many calls trading versus puts, Cboe LiveVol data show.

Meanwhile, metals prices fell across the board, led by a 5% sell-off in copper futures to the lowest since April. Gold futures lost more than 3% to $3,744, the lowest since September 2025, despite Treasury yields slipping on the session.

Almost 300,000 options traded on the SPDR GLD Shares ETF (GLD) Add To Watchlist, with a slight bias towards calls. The catch for gold bugs is that more calls looked like they were initiated by sellers, according to Cboe LiveVol data, which show 52,000 calls sold on the bid versus 45,000 calls bought on the ask.
