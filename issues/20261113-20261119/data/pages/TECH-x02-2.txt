Nvidia’s quarterly earnings call on Tuesday was its first with CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress at the helm alongside the company’s first board chair, Harvey C. Jones. The leadership change followed Jensen Huang’s resignation last week after reports he had multiple affairs – including one with former Megaspeed executive and alleged Chinese asset Alice Huang and one with a direct report, Kimberly Powell. Nvidia is investigating whether Huang’s relationship with the former compromised national security or company secrets. Huang will also testify before lawmakers on December 1st.

Ahead of Tuesday’s earnings, Huang said he would be willing to return as CEO. However, during the company’s earnings call, Jones stated that “the Nvidia board unanimously accepted [Huang’s] resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.” Jones also said that during the call, the company would not be addressing or taking questions “regarding the circumstances of Jensen’s resignation and the ongoing investigation.” He added that, “the Board is proactively dealing with the immediate consequences facing the company, as well as enhancing our oversight capacity going forward.”

In Tuesday’s earnings Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue, continuing years of skyrocketing revenue driven by the AI industry.
