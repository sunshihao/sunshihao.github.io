Anthropic Add To Watchlist jumped 17% in its Nasdaq debut on Thursday in a seminal moment for artificial intelligence, after the company completed the biggest initial public offering on record, topping SpaceX' Add To Watchlist s historic debut in June. The stock closed at $97.17 per share. The Claude creator, trading under the ticker "ANT," opened at $111.50, after the company raised $81.3 billion in the IPO. Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83 a piece on Wednesday, lifting the size and price of its offering.

At Thursday's close, Anthropic had a fully diluted market cap of roughly $1.8 trillion. More than 846 million shares changed hands, surpassing the 522 million shares that were traded on SpaceX's opening day. 

The 5-year-old company captivated the corporate world with its Claude AI models and products, which have fueled its meteoric rise this year. Anthropic said in its prospectus that it generated $33.24 billion in revenue for the nine months ended Sept. 30, up a staggering 1,200% from the same period last year.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei co-founded the company in 2021 after he and a small group of researchers defected from OpenAI, now its chief rival. Anthropic has faced sharp criticism over its positions on safety, regulation and national security, including by President Donald Trump and other senior government officials.
