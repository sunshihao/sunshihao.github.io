# Treasury yields inch higher as investors weigh the state of the U.S. economy
U.S. Treasury yields crawled higher on Thursday as investors digested fresh economic data and the minutes from the Federal Reserve's October meeting.
The benchmark [10-year Treasury](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US10Y/) note yield rose almost 2 basis points to 5.085%, while the yields on the [30-year Treasury](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US30Y/) bond and [2-year Treasury](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US2Y/) note were up more than 1 basis point at 5.426% and more than 2 basis points at 4.826%, respectively.
