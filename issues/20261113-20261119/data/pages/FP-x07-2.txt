# Lawmakers set date for Nvidia CEO to testify

The hearing will allow lawmakers to ask questions of former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang in public, in real time.

November 17, 2026

Jensen Huang in December 2025. (Evelyn Hockstein/Reuters)

By Miriam Waldvogel

Ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has agreed to testify before three congressional committees on Dec. 1, lawmakers said Tuesday, setting the stage for the first live questions from lawmakers since Huang’s stunning downfall last week.
