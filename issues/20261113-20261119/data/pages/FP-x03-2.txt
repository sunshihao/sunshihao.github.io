# Ukraine cripples Crimean road bridge in blow to Putin before meeting with Xi and Trump

By Lina Golovnya and Andrew Osborn

KYIV/MOSCOW, Nov 17 (Reuters) - Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section of the bridge linking Russia and Crimea on Tuesday, ​inflicting a military and symbolic blow on President Vladimir Putin two days before he is due to meet the leaders of China and the US.

Videos and images posted on X by Ukrainian President ‌Volodymyr Zelenskiy and verified by Reuters showed the bridge's main road arch had crumbled into the sea, leaving a yawning gap after two strikes in as many days.

"Russia strikes our infrastructure every day, including bridges," Zelenskiy said. "We are ... reminding Russia that war will always return to where it began."

Speaking later in his nightly video address, Zelenskiy said the assault was carried out by the Ukraine Security Service and all of Ukraine's forces.

"We have long been telling Russia: Enough of striking bridges, cities and villages, energy infrastructure, railways and grain ships. Enough of war," Zelenskiy said. "But ​every time Russia chooses further escalation."

The 19-km (12-mile) bridge is a flagship project inaugurated by Putin in 2018 and a symbol of Russia's attachment to the peninsula it captured and annexed from Ukraine in 2014.

The timing was awkward ​for the Kremlin leader ahead of his meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday with US President Donald Trump and China's Xi Jinping, where the three are expected to discuss the Ukraine war ⁠among other topics.

Zelenskiy said the strike followed an unprecedented operation that methodically destroyed Russian air defences, radar systems and electronic warfare capabilities.

Sergei Aksyonov, Crimea's Russian-installed governor, said two people were killed and road traffic had been suspended. Rail ​traffic partially resumed after a pause.

## KREMLIN SAYS RUSSIA WILL REBUILD IT

Putin's spokesman Dmitry Peskov said a repair plan would be prepared, making the bridge "even better than before".

But Russian military bloggers and other experts said repairs would be challenging given Ukraine's capacity for ​further attacks.

"The road bridge won’t be repaired during this war," Finnish military analyst Emil Kastehelmi posted on X. "Ukraine has achieved a victory with both concrete and symbolic implications."
