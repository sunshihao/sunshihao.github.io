# Jensen Huang and NVIDIA Agree to Appear Before Congress 

November 17, 2026 Press Release

Chairmen John Moolenaar, Brian Mast, and Rick Crawford released a statement after former NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang and NVIDIA General Counsel Tim Teter voluntarily agreed to appear before the Select Committee on China, the House Foreign Affairs Committee, and the House Intelligence Committee for transcribed interviews.

“We look forward to hearing from Jensen Huang and Tim Teter on their roles in a potential breach of our national security,” said Moolenaar, Mast, and Crawford. “The American people need answers on Mr. Huang and NVIDIA’s role in allowing dangerous PRC actors to access our most advanced chips, as well as the role of a potential Chinese intelligence asset in this scandal.”

Jensen Huang will appear before the committees on December 1st, and NVIDIA General Counsel Tim Teter will appear on December 2nd, both for transcribed interviews.
