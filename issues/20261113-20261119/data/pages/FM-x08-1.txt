SAN JOSE, Calif. – November 19, 2026 – BILL (NYSE: BILL), the intelligent finance platform trusted by nearly 500,000 businesses to manage, move, and maximize their money, today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by TPG, a leading global alternative asset management firm, in partnership with Corpay (NYSE: CPAY), the Global Spend Management Company. BILL will be acquired for $63.25 per share in a cash transaction that values BILL at $6.0 billion. TPG will acquire a majority interest in BILL through TPG Capital, the firm’s U.S. and European private equity platform, and Corpay will acquire a minority interest. 

The per share purchase price represents a 31% premium to the 60-day volume-weighted average price ending on November 16, the last full trading day preceding media reports that BILL was in acquisition discussions. 

“This transaction marks an exciting new chapter for BILL, delivering significant, certain, and immediate value to our stockholders, while creating new opportunities to accelerate our transformation and deliver greater value to customers,” said René Lacerte, CEO and Founder of BILL. “With our new partners, we will have the resources, expertise, and strategic support to accelerate innovation, advance our AI transformation, and continue to drive the future of financial operations. We look forward to the opportunities ahead for our customers and our business.”

“BILL has built a differentiated financial operations platform that helps small and midsize businesses manage their most critical financial workflows. We have long recognized the significant opportunity at the intersection of financial technology, AI, and payments, and believe BILL is well positioned to capitalize on these trends through its scaled network, integrated technology, and deep customer relationships,” said John Flynn, Partner at TPG. “We are excited to partner with the BILL team, combining TPG’s technology investing and business-building experience with Corpay’s payments expertise to accelerate innovation and support the company's next chapter,” added Tim Millikin, Partner at TPG.
