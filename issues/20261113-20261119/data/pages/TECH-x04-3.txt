# Nvidia creates board committee to oversee export controls

The move signals that the company is in damage control mode as it seeks to "enhance its engagement with relevant government stakeholders."

Nvidia's company headquarters in Santa Clara, California. (Photo by Benjamin Fanjoy/Getty Images) | 2026 Getty Images

By Sophia Cai and Brian Dabbs

11/17/2026 5:33 AM MST 

Nvidia’s board has established a committee to oversee the company’s national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risks, the company announced Tuesday.

The move comes days after Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia’s CEO amid reports of multiple extramarital affairs that rocked Washington and Silicon Valley, including with alleged Chinese spy Alice Huang. Nvidia’s board is not considering reinstating him, POLITICO reported Monday.

Nvidia’s National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee will be chaired by Director Suzanne Nora Johnson, currently a member of the audit committee that oversees regulatory compliance. Directors Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri will also serve as members.

“Advanced silicon technologies and the emergence of artificial and superintelligence present unprecedented challenges for national security,” board chair Harvey Jones said in a statement. The board said its decision was a commitment “to enhance its engagement with relevant government stakeholders, and to demonstrate the highest standards of governance and oversight.”
