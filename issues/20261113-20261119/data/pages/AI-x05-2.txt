OpenAI releases its updated GPT-6.2 Astra model.

The company says the latest version of its frontier model is better at creating slides, spreadsheets, and documents that “read less like AI,” while more closely following templates. It’s also better at coding when it comes to websites, gaming, and 3D design.

OpenAI is also launching an update to the second-most-powerful model in its lineup, GPT-6.2 Sol, which the company says is “smarter and more efficient” than its predecessor.
