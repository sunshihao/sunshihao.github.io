# Xi says Asia-Pacific needs open trade and Putin condemns sanctions as Trump arrives in China

Chinese President Xi Jinping on Wednesday called on Asia-Pacific economies to uphold free and open trade and deepen cooperation in the digital economy and AI, as the region faces mounting economic uncertainties and challenges to global supply chains.

Updated 2:58 AM CST, November 18, 2026

SHENZHEN, China (AP) — The Asia-Pacific must pursue more open trade and equitable growth if it is to remain stable and dynamic, Chinese President Xi Jinping told business executives gathered Wednesday in the southern Chinese technology and financial hub of Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the annual Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

U.S. President Donald Trump arrived Wednesday for the APEC leaders’ summit, traveling from the Philippines, where he attended an annual summit of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations. 

Trump’s “America First” approach of tariff hikes and other moves runs counter to APEC’s aim of eventually forming a Pacific Rim free trade zone. U.S. limits on Chinese access to leading-edge American technology, such as the Nvidia computer chips that help to power artificial intelligence, are a constant source of strain with Beijing. 

Xi and other APEC leaders called for a more open approach, without directly criticizing the U.S. shift toward protecting its own markets. 

## APEC summit venue showcases China’s central role in trade

The summit is showcasing Shenzhen, which China has transformed in the past four decades from a fishing village into a skyscraper-studded center for export manufacturing and technology. China’s ascent as the second-largest economy hinged on global investments and access to world markets as global manufacturers shifted factories overseas to tap into cheaper labor and faster-growing markets. 

“China will not only cut tariffs and expand market access, but also go a step further. We will carry out reform in challenging areas such as rules, regulation, and standards,” Xi told the CEO summit, reiterating Beijing’s pledges to improve conditions for global investors. 

“While pursuing its own development, China has shared opportunities with other countries and worked for common development,” he said. “It has steadily brought stability and predictability to world economic growth and international trade and economic cooperation.”
