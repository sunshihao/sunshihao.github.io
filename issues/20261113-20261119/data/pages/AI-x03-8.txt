# APEC focuses on safe, responsible AI use, China's ex-UN envoy says 

APEC countries are focused on the safe deployment and responsible application of artificial intelligence, Chen Xu, the chair of the APEC Senior Officials' Meeting this year, said on Tuesday (Nov 17).

 

 People wait for a light show to begin as a building is illuminated with "APEC China" ahead of the APEC summit in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, China, November 15, 2026. REUTERS/Go Nakamura 

SHENZHEN: Countries making up the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) are focused on the safe deployment and responsible application of artificial intelligence, Chen Xu, the chair of the APEC Senior Officials' Meeting this year, said on Tuesday (Nov 17).

APEC members have had an in-depth discussion and reached an important consensus around AI-related issues, said Chen, who was China's ambassador to the United Nations until last year.

His comments come as China and the United States, the world's two largest economies, are in a race over leadership in AI while their technology ties grow more strained.

"In the realms of digital technology and AI, the parties engaged in in-depth discussions on a wide range of issues including... the safe deployment and responsible application of AI," Chen told a press conference.

He added the member states had discussed for the first time the role of data in driving AI growth.

The US on Monday tightened restrictions aimed at preventing Chinese companies from accessing advanced US AI chips through overseas data centers, a loophole Washington says could allow firms to circumvent existing export controls.
