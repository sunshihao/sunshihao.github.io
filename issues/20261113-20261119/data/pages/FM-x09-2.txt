### Walmart U.S. 

 Q3 FY27 Walmart U.S. performance reflected strong momentum as we leaned into greater value and faster delivery. Sales grew across categories, supported by share gains and eCommerce growth of 23%, including nearly 50% growth in marketplace sales. Walmart Connect advertising grew 36% (ex-VIZIO and Vibe). Sales were partially offset by a net headwind of ~70 bps to comp sales in health and wellness, driven by deflation related to Maximum Fair Price Regulation. 

 23% Walmart U.S. eCommerce growth 

 

### Sam's Club U.S. 

 Sam’s Club U.S. delivered strong results in Q3 FY27, driven by growth in transactions and unit volumes, with strength across grocery and general merchandise. Ecommerce sales increased 30%, supported by continued growth in club-fulfilled pickup and delivery. Membership fee revenue grew 2.2%, reflecting steady growth in member counts and Plus penetration, partially offset by the allocation of fees from the bundled membership offering. 

 30% Sam’s Club U.S. eCommerce growth 

 

### Walmart International 

 In Q3 FY27, Walmart International sales grew over 3% in constant currency, reflecting strong growth of 28% in China, partially offset by the impact of the timing shift for Flipkart’s Big Billion Days. Membership fee revenue grew with continued momentum in the Sam’s Club businesses in China and Mexico.
