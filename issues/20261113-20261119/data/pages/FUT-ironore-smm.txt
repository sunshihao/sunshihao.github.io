# [SMM Iron Ore] Bearish macro sentiment and short selling drag iron ore down over 2%

Published: Nov 17, 2026 17:19 (GMT+8) Source: SMM

Iron ore futures extended their weakness today. The DCE most-traded I2701 contract closed at 640.5 yuan/mt, down 2.44% from the previous trading day. Spot prices at Qingdao port fell by about 5-7 yuan/mt on average. Traders were firm on offers, steel mills bought mainly on an as-needed basis and spot trading was average.

On the macro front, US inflation data has kept beating expectations, raising expectations of a Federal Reserve rate hike and lifting the dollar. Commodities are under broad pressure. Funds have kept adding short positions in ferrous futures amid bearish macro sentiment. Open interest in the most-traded iron ore contract rose for two straight sessions as prices fell, climbing from 541,400 lots on 13 November to 607,900 lots. Short selling has amplified bearish fundamentals and added further pressure on prices.

On the demand side, hot metal output lost to blast furnace maintenance was 1.8246 million mt in the week to 13 November, up 96,600 mt week-on-week, according to SMM. Sintering at steel mills in Tangshan has been affected by environmental controls, and hot metal output may fall further. Bearish macro sentiment and short selling are dominating futures trading. Lower arrivals have not changed the picture of high port stocks, while more maintenance and environmental curbs are weighing on raw material demand. Futures and spot prices both weakened. Iron ore prices are likely to stay weak in the short term. Watch for shifts in overseas macro expectations, enforcement of environmental curbs and changes in port stocks.
