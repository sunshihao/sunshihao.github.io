# Xi to APEC Leaders: Asia-Pacific Cooperation Must Not Turn Backward

 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting Shenzhen, People's Republic of China | 19 November 2026 

Chinese President Xi Jinping chaired a meeting of the 21 APEC member economies on Thursday, calling for deeper regional economic integration, freer trade and new sources of growth as global economic growth slows. 

“We must uphold open regionalism, steadily advance economic integration and build a pattern of cooperation that is open rather than exclusive and integrated rather than fragmented,” said President Xi, opening the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting. “We must build an Asia-Pacific community of solidarity and mutual assistance.”

“We are like passengers riding the same ship. To navigate the waves of the Pacific, we must unite as one and look out for each other. We must expand our shared interests, meet the difficulties and challenges together.”

President Xi urged his counterparts that cooperation should advance where conditions allow so that economic development benefits everyone across the region and sectors. 

He highlighted main ideas for an Asia-Pacific community, including the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific (FTAAP) as a shared vision of APEC, calling for closer alignment of the region’s trade agreements, greater trade digitalization, fewer barriers to investment and stronger capacity building to help economies take part in high-standard arrangements.

“Connectivity is a nexus of regional economic integration. In 2014 we jointly developed the APEC Connectivity Blueprint. Today, regional connectivity faces new opportunities.”

Emphasizing greater physical and people-to-people connections, President Xi called for better infrastructure, smarter customs and transport systems, as well as alignment on policies to keep goods, people and supply chains moving smoothly.

He pointed to the digital economy and artificial intelligence as “the engines of innovation-driven growth,” urging stronger digital infrastructure, closer cooperation on data governance and more efficient and secure cross-border data flow.

"We should build an open innovation ecosystem, promote the wide application of artificial intelligence and drive the transformation, upgrading and new development of industries,” he added.

Growth must also withstand new shocks and reach people’s lives, President Xi stressed. “To respond to constantly emerging global challenges, we should promote regional cooperation on energy and food security and deepen cross-border anti-corruption governance,” he said. “We should invest in people and promote employment and vocational skills development.” 

“Economic globalization may have setbacks, but Asia-Pacific cooperation should not turn backward,” President Xi said in his closing remarks.
