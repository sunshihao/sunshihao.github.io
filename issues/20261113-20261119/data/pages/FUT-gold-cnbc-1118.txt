# Gold drops to 14-month low as dollar, yields rise on hawkish Fed rhetoric

Published Tue, Nov 17 2026 10:21 PM EST Updated Wed, Nov 18 2026 1:50 PM EST

Gold prices fell more than 1% on Wednesday to their lowest level in over a year, as a firmer dollar and expectations of higher U.S. interest rates weighed, while investors awaited minutes from the Federal Reserve's meeting for clues on the policy outlook.

Edgar Su | Reuters

Gold sank to ‌a more-than-one-year low on Wednesday as hawkish Federal Reserve signals raised expectations of higher interest rates, boosting the dollar and Treasury yields.

Spot gold Add To Watchlist dropped 2.9% to $3,739.39 per ounce at 1:47 p.m. ET. It ​hit its lowest level since September 26, 2025 earlier in the session at $3,734.76 ​an ounce.

U.S. gold futures Add To Watchlist for December delivery fell 3.2% to $3,741.80 per ounce.

"We ⁠believe the selloff reflects mounting pressure from a strengthening US dollar, rising Treasury yields and ​expectations of further monetary tightening. Yesterday's economic figures reinforced this sentiment," said Naeem Aslam, CIO, ​Zaye Capital Markets.

The dollar strengthened to a fresh 19-month high on Wednesday as hawkish commentary and recent economic data cemented expectations for an interest rate hike next month.

U.S. Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller said on ​Wednesday the central bank needs to get more aggressive in its response to inflation, and ​either approve a half-point hike at the December meeting or communicate a faster pace of interest rate ‌increases ⁠in 2027.

Fed officials John Williams and Alberto Musalem on Tuesday signalled further rate hikes may be needed to curb inflation.

Higher rates increase the opportunity ​cost of holding non-yielding ​gold.

Investors now await the ⁠Fed's October 27-28 meeting minutes due at 1900 GMT.

"If the minutes reinforce concerns about persistent inflation and support additional tightening, Treasury yields ​and the dollar could strengthen further, potentially extending gold's decline below $3,800," ​Aslam added.

Spot ⁠silver Add To Watchlist slipped 4.5% to $45.27 per ounce, after touching its lowest level since September 26, 2025 earlier in the session.

Platinum Add To Watchlist dropped 3.8% to $1,375, after hitting its lowest since September 17, 2025 earlier, ⁠and dropped ​33% so far this year. Palladium slid 1.7% to $954.25, after touching its lowest level since early November.
