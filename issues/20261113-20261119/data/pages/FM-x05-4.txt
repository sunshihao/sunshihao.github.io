# Stocks slip with eyes on Anthropic and Nvidia, oil prices slide

By Rodrigo Campos

November 18, 2026 1:46 AM UTC Updated ago

- Anthropic prices IPO above expected range, source says
- Dollar index hits fresh 19-month high
- Oil prices slide on larger-than-expected inventories

NEW YORK/LONDON, Nov 18 (Reuters) - A gauge of stocks across the globe fell on Wednesday after Nvidia's quarterly results ​and ahead of Anthropic's market debut, while the dollar's strength on the back of a hawkish Federal Reserve ‌weighed on trader sentiment.

The dollar index strengthened to a fresh 19-month high after US Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller told CNBC the central bank needs to either approve a half-point interest rate hike at its December meeting or communicate a faster pace of hikes in 2027.

Mining stocks dragged European ​shares lower, while Nvidia's results initially helped Asian shares, with South Korea's Kospi (.KS11), opens new tab up 2% overnight. But the Nvidia rally fizzled ​and its stock fell 2.9% after rising as much as 2.7%.

Nvidia's board dismissed the idea of rehiring ⁠former CEO Jensen Huang, who resigned last week. The company's results and strong outlook did not dispel uncertainty.

"We believe (Tuesday's) results have stabilized ​sentiment and put a floor under the recent AI selloff, but they have postponed rather than permanently cured investor anxiety," said Neil ​Shah, vice president of research at Counterpoint.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average (.DJI), opens new tab fell 1.11% to 50,198.17, the S&P 500 (.SPX), opens new tab lost 0.23% to 7,323.63 and the Nasdaq Composite (.IXIC), opens new tab rose 0.42% to 24,429.89.

MSCI's gauge of stocks across the globe (.MIWD00000PUS), opens new tab fell 0.45% to 1,074.61.

The next health check on the AI industry will come as Anthropic prepares its ​market debut on Thursday. The company priced its initial public offering on Wednesday at $83 per share, above the expected range, according to a person ‌familiar ⁠with the matter.

## DOLLAR UP, OIL DOWN

In currencies, the dollar strengthened as Waller's comments and recent economic data cemented expectations for an interest rate hike next month.

"The Federal Reserve seems at the moment to be very determined to hike interest rates and maybe even aggressively do so," said Juan Perez, senior director of trading at Monex USA in Washington. "That's the belief right now that's carrying the ​US dollar."
