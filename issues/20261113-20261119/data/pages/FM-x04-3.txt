#### 2026 APEC Leaders’ Shenzhen Outlook

2026 APEC Leaders’ Shenzhen Outlook

Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together

1. We, the Economic Leaders of APEC, gathered in Shenzhen, China, on November 18 to 19, 2026. Decades after APEC’s establishment, our sustained efforts have helped make our region a key engine of global growth and a champion of regional economic cooperation. Under the theme “Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together”, we have built on our work throughout the year with China’s APEC 2026 priorities “Openness, Innovation, Cooperation”, and advanced our shared objectives toward realizing an open, dynamic, resilient, and peaceful Asia-Pacific community, guided by the Putrajaya Vision 2040.

2. The world today is changing rapidly, with new opportunities emerging alongside complex challenges. Technological breakthroughs are contributing to the economic growth, resilience, and dynamism of our region. We endeavor to work together to maintain macroeconomic stability, promote productivity and dynamism, and boost economic growth for the benefit of all our people.

3. APEC has long been at the forefront of regional economic cooperation, and its enduring role remains relevant in the current economic context. We reaffirm our commitment to APEC as the premier forum for regional economic cooperation, guided by its voluntary, non-binding and consensus-building principles, and to making APEC a more modern, efficient, and effective incubator of ideas.

Trade and Investment

4. We underscore that supporting efforts to improve the trade and investment environment is important to our region. We reaffirm our commitments to strengthening supply chain resilience and deepening regional economic cooperation to build robust and resilient economies, contributing to sustained economic growth and prosperity of our region.

5. We will continue advancing economic integration in the Asia-Pacific region in a manner that is market-driven, including through the work on the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific (FTAAP) agenda. We recognize that the FTAAP agenda plays an important role in building economies’ capacity to participate in high standard and comprehensive regional undertakings.

6. We recognize the important role of digitalization as a driver for innovation, productivity, and economic growth across the region, and its benefit to facilitating trade. In this context, we welcome the APEC Trade Digitalization Cooperation Framework (2027-2030) to guide our efforts to foster a supportive policy environment, strengthen policy exchange and promote use of digital technologies for trade digitalization. We will continue to facilitate trade, including through measures to advance paperless trade and cross-border e-commerce. We are also committed to harnessing the benefits of emerging technologies for trade.
