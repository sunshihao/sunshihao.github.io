# Anthropic IPO raises over $81 billion in largest offering on record, topping SpaceX
[Anthropic](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/ANT/) priced its IPO at $83 per share on Wednesday, above the expected range, setting the stage for the Claude creator's eagerly awaited Nasdaq listing and a historic moment for the [artificial intelligence](https://www.cnbc.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/) industry.
