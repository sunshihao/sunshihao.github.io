# Here's what every major Wall Street analyst is saying about Nvidia after its earnings report

Published Wed, Nov 18 2026 7:10 AM EST

Liz Napolitano@LizKNapolitano

Nvidia's latest earnings report inspired confidence among investors on Wall Street, even after the stock's recent plunge due to a spy scandal involving its long-time CEO and top dealmaker, Jensen Huang. The chipmaker posted fiscal third-quarter results that beat the Street. The company also lifted its fiscal 2028 revenue growth forecast to 75% from 70%, despite China being excluded from its data center compute revenue outlook. It also said it expects to bring in $130 billion in revenue for the current quarter, more than the LSEG consensus of $125.1 billion. Shares of Nvidia jumped following the report, last up 3%. Those gains come on the heels of a particularly volatile period for Nvidia. Former CEO Jensen Huang announced his resignation from the company last week after reports of several extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy. Nvidia has lost roughly 24% this month. NVDA mountain 2026-10-29 Shares have fallen about 27% since October 29. Huang was central to securing many of Nvidia's landmark artificial intelligence-related deals, and he navigated the chipmaker through several industry shifts over the course of his more than 30-year tenure at the company. Several Wall Street analysts say the overhang on Nvidia's shares created by the scandal and the subsequent departure of its prolific leader should soon clear up, particularly following the firm's latest earnings report and strong forward-looking commentary. "Although significant uncertainty remains on several fronts, we believe management's commentary should bolster investor confidence in the fundamental outlook for both Nvidia's business and the broader AI ecosystem across a number of dimensions," Goldman Sachs analyst James Schneider said Tuesday in a note to clients. Schneider has a buy rating on Nvidia. He also has a $220 price target on shares, which is nearly 42% above the price at which Nvidia last closed. Here's what else the Street is saying about Nvidia.
