# [SMM Rebar Daily Review] Limited Boost to Market Sentiment, Rebar Prices Consolidate on a Subdued Note

Published: Nov 18, 2026 17:32 (GMT+8)

Rebar futures consolidated today, with the most-traded contract closing at 2,977, down 0.03% from the previous trading day. In the spot market, most regions held quotes steady today, with only some markets lowering offers by 10 yuan/mt to boost shipments. Overall transaction performance was relatively average. From a fundamental perspective, on the supply side, as previously idled rolling lines resumed production one after another, combined with some hot metal being redirected back to construction steel, BF steel mills increased construction steel supply somewhat. For short-process steelmaking, regional performance diverged slightly. In east China, as rebar futures continued to hit bottom, market sentiment remained negative, and with the arrival of a cold wave, end-user willingness to purchase was not strong, so operating hours were slightly reduced. In south China, however, some producers slightly raised electric furnace operating rates to fill out market specifications, resulting in a marginal supply increase overall. On the demand side, the futures decline paused today, but this provided relatively limited support to market sentiment, and downstream end-users still mainly made just-in-time procurement. Overall, as bearish sentiment is gradually digested, futures may have some room to rebound slightly, but given rebar's own lack of upward momentum, the rebound height may be constrained, and prices are likely to continue consolidating at lows in the short term.
