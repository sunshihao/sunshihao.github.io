# Apec leaders agree joint statement with pledges to boost trade, supply chain resilience

Leaders agree to work together to enhance security across energy and technology sectors as Apec summit wraps up in Shenzhen

Sherry Wang in Beijing and Sylvia Ma in Shenzhen

Published: 11:03pm, 19 Nov 2026 Updated: 11:09pm, 19 Nov 2026

Asia-Pacific economies pledged to strengthen supply chain resilience and leverage emerging technologies to boost trade, as they wrapped up a two-day leaders’ meeting in China’s southern tech hub of Shenzhen.

In a joint statement, titled the Shenzhen Outlook, released late on Thursday, leaders of 21 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation member economies reaffirmed their commitment to maintaining supply chain resilience, particularly for essential goods, amid challenges facing the region.

They also pledged to enhance “security across energy sectors and technologies” and work together to ensure “uninterrupted, reliable, affordable and sustainable access to energy” to support economic prosperity, according to the statement.
