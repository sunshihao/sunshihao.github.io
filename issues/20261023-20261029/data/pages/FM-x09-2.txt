# Boeing says it won't significantly ramp up 737 Max production until late 2027
## Third-quarter earnings
For the three months ended Sept. 30, Boeing reported a net loss of $116 million, or 26 cents a share, compared with a $5.34 billion loss, or $7.14 per share, a year earlier. Adjusting for one-time items, the company posted a loss of 34 cents a share. A year earlier, Boeing had taken a nearly $5 billion [charge](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/29/boeing-ba-3q-2025-earnings.html) on its long-delayed 777X aircraft program.
