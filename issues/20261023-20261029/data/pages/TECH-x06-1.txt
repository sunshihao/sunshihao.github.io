# “Bulletproof” website domain seized in international law enforcement operation
Tuesday, October 27, 2026
Share right caret
**For Immediate Release**
U.S. Attorney's Office, Eastern District of Virginia
ALEXANDRIA, Va. – Theophani K. Stamos, First Assistant U.S. Attorney for the Eastern District of Virginia, and Darren B.
Cox, Assistant Director in Charge of the FBI Washington Field Office, announced today the seizure of the website virtualine.net after it was used to commit and facilitate prolific criminal activity, including the advertisement of sexually explicit images of minors, the intentional disclosure of nonconsensual intimate visual depictions of adults and minors, and international money laundering designed to
conceal and promote criminal conduct.
According to court records, the United States obtained court authorization to seize virtualine.net, which is the website for Virtualine, aka Secury, Massgod, Spamhouse, Lanedo, and Omegatech. Virtualine is a “bulletproof” service offering hosting and server solutions with domain name purchasing options. Virtualine advertises its services to criminals on cybercrime forums.
