# DoorDash to Acquire Instacart to Help Local Stores Win
DoorDash, Inc. (NASDAQ: DASH) and Maplebear Inc. (d.b.a. Instacart, NASDAQ: CART) today announced a definitive agreement under which DoorDash will acquire Instacart for a total value of $65.00 for each Instacart share, or an equity value of approximately $17 billion.
The aggregate transaction consideration will be at least 75% cash, with Instacart stockholders able to elect cash, DoorDash stock, or a combination of both, subject to proration so that DoorDash stock does not exceed 25% of the aggregate consideration. Instacart's board of directors has unanimously approved the agreement.
DoorDash and Instacart share a mission to help local businesses succeed. Families turn to Instacart for the big weekly shop and to DoorDash for meals and nearly anything else they need from their neighborhood in under an hour.
The combined company will offer grocers and retailers of every size DoorDash's delivery network and Instacart's grocery and fulfillment technology, in both apps and on stores' own websites, along with the advertising and AI tools each company has built. On either app, customers pick a store in their own community, and the store makes the sale.
Consumers will have more stores to choose from, more savings opportunities when they choose to shop with a local business, and faster delivery options, while Dashers and Shoppers will have more ways to earn.
Most grocery shopping still happens in stores, but more people are buying groceries and everyday goods online. They expect fast, affordable delivery and an easy way to order. The biggest online retailers and grocers are racing ahead, building their own delivery fleets, warehouses, membership programs, and AI shopping assistants.

...

* **Open Platform, No Grocery Exclusivity** : Local grocers can work with any delivery platform they choose to help them grow.
* **Shoppers Keep What They Love** : Instacart Shoppers can keep the features they love, including the flexibility to choose how much they work and the information they need to decide which batches to accept, and choose whether to take on DoorDash orders or keep shopping only on Instacart.
Following closing, DoorDash is expected to keep Instacart’s brand, enterprise technology products, and app. Until the transaction closes, DoorDash and Instacart will continue to operate as separate and independent companies.
