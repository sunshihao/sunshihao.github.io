# News Releases
ARLINGTON, Va. , Oct. 27, 2026 / [PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / --
**_Third Quarter 2026_**
* _737-7 achieved amended type certification (ATC) from the FAA; 737-10 achieved ATC in October_
* _Selected by the U.S. Navy to design, build and deliver the F/A-XX next generation fighter aircraft_
* _Operating cash flow of $1.5 billion and free cash flow (non-GAAP)* of $0.5 billion_
* _Total company backlog grew to a record $745 billion, including over 6,300 commercial airplanes_

...

| *Non-GAAP measure; complete definitions of Boeing's non-GAAP measures are on page 5, "Non-GAAP Measures Disclosures." |
The Boeing Company (NYSE: BA) recorded third quarter revenue of $24.2 billion, primarily reflecting higher defense and services volume and less favorable commercial delivery mix. The company reported GAAP loss per share of ($0.26) and core loss per share (non-GAAP)* of ($0.34). Operating cash flow in the quarter was $1.5 billion and free cash flow (non-GAAP)* was $0.5 billion. Total company backlog at quarter end grew to a record $745 billion.
"Recent milestones, including certification of the 737-7 and 737-10 and selection to design and build the Navy's sixth-generation fighter aircraft, are important steps as we continue building on our progress," said Kelly Ortberg, Boeing president and chief executive officer. "But we are not done.
We remain focused on safety and quality as we redouble our efforts on Lean and disciplined execution to deliver on our commitments and build the durable performance our stakeholders expect."

...

The 737 program continues to progress toward stabilized production of 47 per month. During the quarter, the 737-7 received amended type certification from the Federal Aviation Administration, and the 737-10 received certification in October. The 787 program remains stable at a production rate of 8 per month. In the quarter, the 777X program continued to progress on 777-9 certification flight testing and remains on track for first delivery in 2027.
