### **Will Iran take the latest iteration of the Qatari deal?**
[Qatari mediators](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913092) presented Iran with a new draft deal on Sunday to end the war with the US and Israel, and Tehran is expected to send a response to the draft by the end of the week, an official briefed on the negotiations said on Wednesday.
The new draft Iran is considering sets a seven-day timeline that addresses both Tehran's and Washington's concerns and lays out daily confidence-building steps each side must take, the official said.
The timeline could begin early next week, and several countries would act as guarantors of the deal, the official added.
