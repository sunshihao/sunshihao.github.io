The DOJ is reportedly looking into Nvidia’s Hugging Face acquisition.
The US Department of Justice has “made inquiries” about the $13 billion deal, including how it could affect competition in the AI industry, according to a report from Reuters. It adds to existing regulatory scrutiny surrounding Nvidia, as it’s also reportedly facing a DOJ investigation over its AI chip dominance.
US DOJ is inquiring about Nvidia's purchase of Hugging Face, sources say
