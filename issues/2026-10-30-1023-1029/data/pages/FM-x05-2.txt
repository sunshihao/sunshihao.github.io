# DoorDash to buy Instacart for $17 billion in big consolidation for grocery delivery
The partnership with one of the nation’s largest grocery chains expanded last fall, and DoorDash now lists the full inventory of all 2,700 U.S. Kroger stores in its online marketplace. DoorDash scored another coup last month when it [added delivery from Costco](https://apnews.com/article/doordash-uber-eats-costco-delivery-e6f030d67dfc34fad737282c108ed929) .
