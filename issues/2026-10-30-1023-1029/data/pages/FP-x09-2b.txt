Tehran has received a letter from US Vice President JD Vance but has not yet responded, Iran’s state-linked Tasnim news agency reported on Thursday.
Vance sent the letter to Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of Iran’s Supreme National Security Council, Tasnim reported, without disclosing its contents.
