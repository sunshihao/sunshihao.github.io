# S&P 500, Dow rise for third consecutive day after Fed holds steady on rates: Live updates
## Stocks end Wednesday's session higher
The three leading U.S. indexes finished in the green on Wednesday.
The S&P 500 added 0.28% to 7,743.98, while the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.27% to close at 26,549.85. The Dow Jones Industrial Average moved up 97.53 points, or 0.19%, to 52,475.10.
