# U.S. Treasury yields are little changed as investors look ahead to the Fed's rate decision
The benchmark [10-year Treasury](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US10Y/) yield was little changed at 4.986%, and the [30-year Treasury](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US30Y/) bond yield dipped nearly 2 basis points at 5.299%. The [2-year Treasury](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/US2Y/) note yield rose 3 basis points higher to 4.716%.
