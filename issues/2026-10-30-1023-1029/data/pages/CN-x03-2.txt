# Chinese shares close lower Tuesday
Source: Xinhua
Editor: huaxia
2026-10-27 17:24:19
This file photo shows an exterior view of the Shanghai Stock Exchange in Shanghai, east China. (Xinhua) BEIJING, Oct. 27 (Xinhua) -- Chinese stocks closed lower on Tuesday, with the benchmark Shanghai Composite Index down 1.33 percent to 3,648.3 points. The Shenzhen Component Index closed 2.5 percent lower at 11,245.73 points. Turnover of stocks covered by these two indices totaled about 1.46 trillion yuan (around 217.36 billion U.S.

The STAR Composite Index, which reflects the performance of stocks on China's sci-tech innovation board, closed 3.93 percent lower at 1,511.95 points on Tuesday. ■
