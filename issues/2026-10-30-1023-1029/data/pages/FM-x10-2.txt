# Brazil stocks, real tumble as Lula’s re-election ends bullish run
[Sign up now:](https://www.straitstimes.com/newsletter-signup?ref=inline-article) Get ST's newsletters delivered to your inbox

...

NEW YORK/BRASILIA/LONDON – Brazilian stocks and the real currency slumped after the surprise [re-election of President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/lula-brazils-iconic-working-class-president-wins-historic-fourth-term-in-dramatic-comeback?ref=inline-article) as the bullish investor sentiment which followed his right-wing rival’s success in the first round of voting evaporated.
The Brazilian real tumbled nearly 12% against the US dollar in early spot trading, one of its biggest daily moves on record, while Bovespa stock index futures fell nearly 15% and interest rate futures rose.
The sell-off hit Brazil’s sovereign debt, with longer-dated bonds hit hardest and the 2056 maturity down around 2.5 cents.
Meanwhile, trading on Brazil’s stock exchange was halted for 30 minutes after the benchmark Ibovespa index fell 10% from its last close, triggering the market’s circuit breaker mechanism, stock exchange operator B3 said in a statement.
The move came after B3 had initially expanded the daily trading limits for Ibovespa stock index futures, dollar futures and interest rate futures in response to heightened market volatility.
