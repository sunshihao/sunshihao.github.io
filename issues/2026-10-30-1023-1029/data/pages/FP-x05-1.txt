# Back in Venezuela, opposition leader endorses U.S. plan for elections

María Corina Machado, who recently returned from exile, called the country’s transition to democracy “irreversible.”

October 29, 2026 at 6:53 p.m. EDT Today at 6:53 p.m. EDT

Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado addresses supporters in Caracas on Thursday. (Gaby Oraa/Reuters)

Samantha Schmidt

CARACAS, Venezuela — Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado, making her first public address since her surprise return to the country, endorsed U.S. plans for free elections as soon as next year, a transition to democracy that she called “irreversible.”
