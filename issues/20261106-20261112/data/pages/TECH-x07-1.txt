# TSMC October 2026 Revenue Report

Issued by: TSMC

Issued on:

[TSMC October 2026 Revenue Report](/system/files/newspdf/attachment/382be7eb993b44afd73aa6fbcf92ac792433c7d1/October%202026%20%28E%29_final_wmn.pdf)

**HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C. – November 10, 2026 -** TSMC (TWSE: 2330, NYSE: TSM) today announced its net revenue for October 2026: On a consolidated basis, revenue for October 2026 was approximately NT$586.89 billion, an increase of 14.7 percent from September 2026 and an increase of 59.7 percent from October 2025. Revenue for January through October 2026 totaled NT$4,485.62 billion, an increase of 43.3 percent compared to the same period in 2025.
