# US Justice Department has not concluded Alice Huang was Chinese spy, Blanche says 

- Nov 12, 2026 
- 3 hrs ago

 

 

 

 FILE PHOTO: A screengrab from CTI video footage obtained November 9, 2026 shows a woman identified by Reuters as Alice Huang after comparison with a photograph obtained separately, attending a dinner with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang (in black) at a restaurant in Taipei, Taiwan May 22, 2025. CTI via REUTERS TV CTI 

WASHINGTON, Nov 12 (Reuters) - US Attorney General Todd Blanche said on Thursday that the Justice Department has not yet reached a conclusion on whether Alice Huang, the Chinese businesswoman who had an affair with former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, was a Chinese spy.

Blanche told reporters at a press conference that "of course" the FBI was investigating reports that Alice Huang was working for the Chinese government. 

(Reporting by Dan Rosenzweig-Ziff and Andrew Goudsward.)
