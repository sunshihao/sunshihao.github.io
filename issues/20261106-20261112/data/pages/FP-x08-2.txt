# 2 top DOJ officials resign, including assistant attorney general for national security

John Eisenberg and Jason Quiñones are leaving the agency.

![Image 1: John Eisenberg appears before a Senate Committee on Intelligence hearing for his pending confirmation to be assistant attorney general for the NSD, on Capitol Hill.](https://www.politico.com/dims4/default/resize/630/quality/90/format/webp?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.politico.com%2Fb4%2Fd5%2Fb5ff25744cbda8cdfca3697ebcc4%2Fsenate-intelligence-62215.jpg)

Two top Justice Department officials resigned Friday, including the assistant attorney general for national security, just a day after the department’s [civil rights division chief departed](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/05/justice-department-civil-rights-division-harmeet-dhillon) her post.

John Eisenberg, the assistant attorney general in charge of DOJ’s national security division, resigned Friday, according to two people familiar with the matter who were granted anonymity because they weren’t authorized to speak publicly. He oversaw prosecutions of terrorism, leak investigations and espionage cases, and his division [was part of the prosecution](https://www.justice.gov/nsd/staff-profile/assistant-attorney-general-john-eisenberg) of former national security adviser John Bolton[for illegally retaining](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/06/26/john-bolton-pleads-guilty-classified-documents-00977972) classified information from his tenure in the first Trump White House.

Eisenberg[caught Washington’s attention](https://www.politico.com/news/2019/10/16/lawyer-ukraine-impeachment-trump-048755) in the first Trump administration as one of the central figures in the White House’s handling of President Donald Trump’s call with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy when he was the National Security Council’s top lawyer. He’s known as a quiet and unassuming presence in a Trump administration dominated by more colorful personalities.

A spokesperson for DOJ and Eisenberg didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment.
