# Top intel Dem unsure if Alice Huang is a Chinese spy or ‘useful idiot’ after classified briefing

Rep. Jim Himes (D-Conn.) told POLITICO that intelligence leaders "didn't know" whether the FBI investigated Alice Huang after concerns were raised earlier this year about her relationship with Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang.

![Image 1: Rep. Jim Himes (R-Conn.) speaks during a House Intelligence Committee hearing featuring Marie Yovanovitch, former U.S. ambassador to Ukraine, as part of the impeachment inquiry into President Donald Trump on Capitol Hill in Washington, D.C., on Nov. 15, 2019.](https://www.politico.com/dims4/default/resize/630/quality/90/format/webp?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.politico.com%2F19%2Fbd%2F407a2d8b49b0ba0a4b4bfe48e42b%2Fhttps-delivery-gettyimages.com%2Fdownloads%2F1182589261)

Rep. Jim Himes (D-Conn.), the likely incoming chair of the House Intelligence Committee, is unsure whether a business executive who cultivated close ties with Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang is a Chinese spy, based on information he received from U.S. intelligence officials in a classified briefing earlier this week.

In a wide-ranging interview on Wednesday, Himes told POLITICO he left the briefing given by heads of U.S. intelligence agencies to top lawmakers on the House and Senate intelligence committees without clarity on whether Alice Huang knowingly cultivated information for the Chinese government on the $4 trillion U.S. chip company, or was a “useful idiot” unaware of China’s wider espionage activities.

“What’s the relationship with Alice Huang and the Chinese, and what do we know about what influence or information was traded? Because today, at least, I don’t know,” he said.

He noted that the classified briefing touched on multiple subjects, including the Nvidia CEO’s relationship with Huang, who is associated with Singapore-based data center company Megaspeed.
