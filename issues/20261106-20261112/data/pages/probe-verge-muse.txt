Meta is launching its “smallest and most efficient” AI model: Muse Ember.

The new model is “designed for complex reasoning execution and the work agents repeat all day: tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long document parsing,” Meta says.

The company claims Muse Ember delivers “more intelligence per dollar” than Anthropic’s Claude Haiku 5.5 or OpenAI’s GPT-6 Luna “at any reasoning setting.”
