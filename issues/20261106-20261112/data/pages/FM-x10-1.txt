November 9, 2026

Highlights 

• Paramount Skydance Q3 1 (excludes WBD)

◦ Paramount Skydance Q3 net earnings was $73 million and adj. EBITDA was $1.0 billion, a 14% margin on revenue of $7.0 billion, which grew 5% year-over-year. Paramount remains on track for the high end of its 2026 profitability outlook at $3.9 billion.

◦ Paramount+ revenue grew 21%, its fastest quarterly growth this year, with ARPU up approximately 14%. Studios revenue grew 23% and TV Media achieved a 34% adj. EBITDA margin.

• Skydance (combined company)

◦ Completed the acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery on October 6, 2026.

◦ Targeting at least $6 billion of annualized run-rate synergies within three years, with progress underway across more than 20 workstreams.

◦ Executing our operational plan to reduce net leverage ratio to 3.0x by the end of 2029 and reach investment-grade credit metrics. 

◦ Planning to launch a bundled offering of HBO Max and Paramount+ in early 2027.

◦ Skydance produced 7 of the top 10 most watched series across linear and streaming 2 and the top 4 original titles on streaming. 3 Across Paramount and Warner Bros. platforms, Skydance is the second largest media distributor by share of total U.S. TV time, per Nielsen’s The Gauge. 4 

◦ DTC Q3 pro forma revenue grew 15%. 5

◦ In Q4, we expect continued strong DTC revenue growth, Skydance TV trends to remain consistent with recent quarters, and the improved Paramount film slate results to be more than offset by underperformance of the Warner Bros. film slate.
