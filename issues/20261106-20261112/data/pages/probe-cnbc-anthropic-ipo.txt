# Anthropic sets IPO price range of $70 to $78, looks to start trading Nov. 19, sources say

Key Points

- Anthropic said it will offer 825 million shares in its upcoming IPO at a price range between $70 and $78 per share.
- Should the offering take place in mid-November as expected, it will come roughly five months after SpaceX's record IPO. 
- Anthropic has filed to trade on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol "ANT."

Anthropic said it will offer 825 million shares in its upcoming IPO at a price range between $70 and $78 per share, according to an updated filing on Tuesday, bringing the 5-year-old artificial intelligence lab a step closer to a potentially historic Nasdaq debut. 

The company is planning to price on Nov. 18 and trade on Nov. 19, according to people familiar with the details who were granted anonymity because they were not authorized to speak publicly. Anthropic is targeting retail allocation of about 20%, the people said, a much higher percentage than the typical initial public offering, which is 5% to 10% for retail investors.

At the high end of the range, Anthropic would net up to $64.35 billion in proceeds through the IPO. The company could end up being worth up to $1.405 trillion on a fully diluted basis, rising from the $965 billion valuation it announced as part of a funding round in May.

The shares sold in the private placement will not be registered in the offering and will not be subject to lock-up agreements with the underwriters, the company said.

As Anthropic executives get set to pitch the offering to investors, they have to contend with a tumultuous political environment, following last week's midterm elections that included a Democratic sweep in Congress and the passage of numerous statewide ballot measures that could slow the AI buildout. They're also facing a public market that's grown increasingly skeptical of the AI trade on concern that rising interest rates will make debt unaffordable for companies that need it for costly infrastructure purchases. 

CNBC reported last week that some Anthropic investors were bracing for a lower valuation than had previously been expected. While Anthropic had been looking toward a $2 trillion valuation until recently, shareholders told CNBC that even $1.5 trillion is now in doubt, with one investor calling that number a "stretch."
