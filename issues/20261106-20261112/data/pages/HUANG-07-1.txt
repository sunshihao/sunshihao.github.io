# Statement From the Independent Members of the NVIDIA Board

November 10, 2026

  [![Statement From the Independent Members of the NVIDIA Board](https://iprsoftwaremedia.com/219/files/202512/692f56983d6332f89cbbc58b_nvidia-logo-horiz-wht-16x9/nvidia-logo-horiz-wht-16x9_mid.png?v=44a60e3f-c4e5-4d8a-879f-ec5121ad6f4b "Statement From the Independent Members of the NVIDIA Board")](https://iprsoftwaremedia.com/219/files/202512/nvidia-logo-horiz-wht-16x9.png)  

The independent members of the NVIDIA Board have retained former acting U.S. Attorney of the Southern District of New York Joon H. Kim and his firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct a thorough and independent investigation as was announced on Sunday.
