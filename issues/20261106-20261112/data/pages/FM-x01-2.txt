The Nasdaq Composite sank 5.5% into correction territory on Thursday, while the S&P 500 fell about 4.4% and the Dow dwindled roughly 1,833 points, or 3.4%.

AI-linked technology shares led the decline as investors worried that data-center developments could slow after the midterm election results.

The S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq posted their steepest one-day declines since the April 2025 tariff shock.

Stocks suffered a major sell-off on Thursday as investors' fears about a slowdown in the buildout of artificial intelligence rocked not only the trade itself but also the broader market.

The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite receded more than 5% and finished in correction territory alongside the small-cap Russell 2000 index. The blue-chip Dow Jones Industrial Average retreated more than 1,800 points, or more than 3%, and the broad market S&P 500 pulled back more than 4%.

Here are the most breathtaking statistics from the day's rout:

The Nasdaq Composite posted its worst performance in a single day since April 4, 2025, when it fell 5.82%

The S&P 500 notched its worst single-day performance since April 4, 2025, when it declined 5.97%

The Dow Jones Industrial Average secured its worst day since April 4, 2025, when it dropped more than 2,200 points, or 5.5%

The "Magnificent Seven" lost more than $1 trillion in aggregate market cap, with Nvidia leading the way at a loss of more than $440 billion

Nvidia fell more than 9% and turned negative on the year, with its year-to-date fall now at 2.6%

On a point basis, the CBOE Volatility Index (VIX) — which hit its highest level since March 31 — gained 11.57 points for the biggest point gain since April 4, 2025, when the VIX gained 15.29 points

On a percentage basis, the VIX closed up 69.66% for the third-biggest gain on record back to 1990

On the New York Stock Exchange, declining stocks outpaced advancing stocks 2,344 to 416, or almost 6 to 1

On the Nasdaq Composite, declining stocks outpaced advancing stocks 4,166 to 855, or almost 5 to 1

All 11 S&P 500 sectors were negative, led to the downside by information technology, down 7.30% to post its worst daily performance since March 16, 2020, when it fell 13.92%

Industrials fell 4.12%, its worst daily performance since April 4, 2025, when it fell 6.29%

310, or 61.6%, of stocks in the S&P 500 are 10% or more below the 52-week high

59, or 58.4%, of stocks in the Nasdaq-100 are more than 20% off the 52-week high, including Rocket Lab, CoreWeave, Arm Holdings and SpaceX, among others

72, or 71.3%, of stocks in the Nasdaq-100 are more than 10% off the 52-week high
