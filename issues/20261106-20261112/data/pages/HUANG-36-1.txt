# Chinese ‘spy’ Alice Huang met Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang years earlier — and was introduced through her tycoon husband

By

[Vivi Lin](https://nypost.com/author/vivi-lin/) and

[Emily Lefroy](https://nypost.com/author/emily-lefroy/)

Published  Nov. 10, 2026, 7:07 p.m. ET

See more of our coverage in your search results.

[IMAGE]

SAN FRANCISCO — Suspected Chinese spy Alice Huang met Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang a decade before their illicit affair was made public, according to a report — and she was introduced through her corporate raider husband.

The $150 billion AI chip boss had dealings with Alice’s husband Kang Weigh in the 2010s when he was buying up Chinese video game companies, a source who knows the couple told [The Information.](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jensen-huangs-yearslong-ties-alice-huang-enabled-nvidia-chip-sales-china)

Gaming was a major source of demand for Nvidia’s high-powered chips before the AI boom turned the company into a $4.5 trillion behemoth.

[![Alice Huang and Jensen Huang standing outdoors near a building with string lights overhead.](https://nypost.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/alice-huang-jensen-huang-no-148138520_e7651f.jpg?w=693)  5](# "Open a slideshow of all 5 article images.")

Alice Huang met Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang a decade before their illicit affair was made public. Obtained by NY Post

Earlier reports had Alice meeting Jensen in early 2024 when she started working to buy AI chips from Nvidia. Jensen admitted Sunday to cheating on his wife of 40 years.

Chinese media reports suggest that Kang and Alice leveraged their relationship with Jensen over the years to drive business.

Some posts claimed that Kang was showing off Alice’s relationship with Jensen to their clients.

Alice posted pics together with Jensen — frequently quaffing wine from overlarge goblets at trendy restaurants — on her WeChat social media.

The Information reported that Alice’s closeness to the Nvidia boss, who is now being investigated by both the FBI and his own company, was her biggest selling point in ginning up business in China.

Alice’s most recent dealings with Jensen came via the company Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center firm that did billions of dollars in business with Nvidia.

[![Alice Huang and Jensen Huang drink wine together.](https://nypost.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/alice-jensen-huang-drinking-png-148137733_67eb01.jpg?w=1024)  5](# "Open a slideshow of all 5 article images.")

Alice Huang and Jensen Huang drink together in an undated photo. Obtained by NY Post

Megaspeed has also been accused of smuggling high end chips to China — in violation of a US export ban — with 50,000 reportedly turning up missing during one audit.

Megaspeed denies any wrongdoing and said it follows all regulations. The Chinese embassy has furiously denied claims that Alice Huang was working as a spy.

Before joining Megaspeed, Alice was a senior executive and equity partner at Shanghai Hexi Investment, a firm with a history of regulatory penalties.

In 2018, Kang’s firm was fined $2.6 million, with Kang personally fined $60,000 for disclosure violations and illegal trading during a restricted period linked to the takeover of Huiqiu Technology, [according to published regulatory notices.](https://finance.sina.cn/stock/ywgg/2018-02-06/detail-ifyreyvz9541300.d.html)

In 2025, the [China Securities Regulatory Commission](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101928/c7576313/content.shtml?utm_source%3Dchatgpt.com&source=gmail&ust=1794347934260000&usg=AOvVaw2qOUFFUF3EtWxKK5v1QAPL) fined Hexi another $350,000 for failing to disclose an insider stake in a company it acquired.

[![Alice Huang smiling while sitting and leaning against a white wall outdoors, wearing a white t-shirt and jeans.](https://nypost.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/827d344d-074d-4045-b480-1efd6fc3e964-148224676_d34aff.jpg?w=923)  5](# "Open a slideshow of all 5 article images.")

Alice Huang smiling while sitting and leaning against a white wall outdoors, wearing a white t-shirt and jeans in a social media post. Obtained by NY Post

In images uncovered by The Post on Tuesday, the [a](http://obtain business.)[ccused spy](https://nypost.com/2026/11/09/business/what-suspected-spy-alice-huang-could-have-extracted-during-pillow-talk-with-nvidia-boss/) can be seen flaunting her access to Jensen on her Chinese social media account, repeatedly posting photos with him.

Alice — who started her career in TV and then moved to finance — appeared to have no experience working in AI before 2023, when Megaspeed was established, according to the report.

[![Alice Huang and Jensen Huang in office.](https://nypost.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/alice-huang-jensen-huang-office-148233151_e83e90.jpg?w=1024)  5](# "Open a slideshow of all 5 article images.")

Alice Huang, left, and Jensen Huang gather in an office. Obtained by NY Post

However, the executive reportedly quickly rose to a position of power by flaunting her [tight-knit relationship with Jensen.](https://nypost.com/2026/10/29/business/alleged-chinese-spy-had-close-relationship-with-nvidia-boss-jensen-huang-report/)

The New York Times reported that Alice and Jensen rendezvoused around the globe over the past three years, including in Shanghai; at Nvidia’s Silicon Valley headquarters; at a golf resort south of San Jose, California; and in Taiwan.

The [suspected spy](https://nypost.com/2026/11/08/tech/jensen-huang-ceo-of-4-5t-chipmaker-nvidia-admits-affair-as-memo-warned-lover-was-chinese-spy/) also bragged that Jensen celebrated her birthday every year by having dinner with her or sending her gifts, people who know Alice told The Information.

An Nvidia China sales employee also claimed that Alice attended the company’s yearly gathering for local customers in China.

[![Alice Huang and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang at a dinner in Taipei.](https://nypost.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/2026-opened-probe-huang-acknowledged-148080068_484421.jpg?w=1024)  5](# "Open a slideshow of all 5 article images.")

Alice Huang, right, attends a dinner hosted by Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang, left, in Taipei on May 22, 2025, in this screengrab from CTI video. CTI/AFPTV/AFP via Getty Images

While little is known about the alleged spy’s private life, before entering the world of finance, she had worked as a reporter for CCTV, the national broadcaster of the Chinese state, and Hunan TV, a regional broadcaster in central China, according to her LinkedIn biography.
