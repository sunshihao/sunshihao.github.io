# Markets News, Nov. 12, 2026: Major Stock Indexes Fall as Investors Digest News of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s Exit; AMD, Broadcom Soar

 The U.S. stock market has been on a volatile run lately amid uncertainty about the sustainability of the AI trade that has propelled the market to record highs in recent years. Michael Nagle / Bloomberg / Getty Images 

 Stocks closed lower Thursday after a volatile day of trading, as investors processed the news that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, the most prominent executive in the AI era, has stepped down. 

 The tech-focused Nasdaq Composite finished the day down 0.4%, after falling as much as 1.6% in the opening minutes of trading, while the S&P 500 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average also closed 0.4% lower. The major indexes were coming off a winning session on Wednesday that was sparked in part by a rebound in Nvidia (NVDA) shares, which had plunged to start the week following revelations that Huang was being investigated by the company’s board over an extramarital relationship he had with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy. 

 Nvidia shares fell as much as 14% early Thursday—but managed to pare that loss to 4.3% by the close—after the company announced that Huang had resigned, effective immediately, and that CFO Colette Kress would take over as CEO on an interim basis. The news came after The Wall Street Journal reported late yesterday that Nvidia’s board was preparing to dismiss Huang, who founded the company in 1993 and has served as CEO ever since. That report came on the heels of another by The New York Times that said Huang also recently had an affair with an Nvidia executive. 

 Shares of two major Nvidia rivals paced the broader market’s recovery from steep early losses. Broadcom (AVGO) and Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) each climbed nearly 6% to lead S&P 500 advancers. Other chip stocks were under pressure, with Intel (INTC), Marvell Technology (MRVL) and Micron (MU) each down roughly 2%. The iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) ticked lower after surging 6% the previous session. 

 Shares of a few of the companies that Nvidia invests in plunged today. CoreWeave shares (CRWV), which soared 25% yesterday following a strong quarterly earnings report from the cloud computing platform, fell 7%, while Nebius Group (NBIS) shares dropped 17% today after the AI cloud company released its quarterly results. Shares of optics and laser maker Lumentum Holdings (LITE) declined 4%. 

 Shares of Super Micro Computer (SMCI) tumbled 11%, following a report that the server maker and Nvidia partner is the target of a Department of Justice investigation.
