# Businessman Who Introduced Alice to Jensen Was Her Husband

Kang Wei, Alice Huang's spouse, facilitated her NVIDIA CEO connection amid chip export investigations

[By Lee Chul-min](/english/people/lee-chulmin/)

Published 2026.11.12. 15:24
Updated 2026.11.12. 16:39

![Kang Wei-Ellis Huang couple 2013](https://www.chosun.com/resizer/v2/VKSP4LCXFFBZ7MCYM32FMQ66XI.jpg?auth=5c92bad490d2f78083215250a17bd49f8d931d007449e24b5640391c83567058&width=616)

Kang Wei-Ellis Huang couple 2013

The person who introduced Alice Huang to Jensen Huang was Kang Wei, a Chinese businessman who had been in a business relationship with Jensen Huang for 10 years. However, according to sources familiar with the couple, Kang Wei was coincidentally Alice Huang’s husband, the Wall Street Journal reported on the 12th.

According to acquaintances close to the couple Kang Wei and Alice Huang, Kang appeared to tolerate his wife’s romantic relationship with Jensen Huang for about two years and even openly spoke about their close relationship. He reportedly introduced his wife as Jensen Huang’s personal secretary in a semi-joking manner.

The WSJ reported, “The anecdote of introducing his wife to the CEO at the center of the global AI boom is the most vivid example of how the Kang and Huang couple leveraged their network to expand their businesses in China, Singapore, and Taiwan.”

Over the past decade, Alice Huang and Kang Wei moved between profitable sectors such as video game companies, pharmaceutical mergers and acquisitions, and cloud computing, building complex networks, transferring shares among themselves, and capitalizing on all available connections. NVIDIA’s AI semiconductor chips were reportedly their most recent venture, following trends in lucrative investments and clashing with regulators.

U.S. authorities suspect that Chinese intelligence agencies recruited Alice Huang as an informant due to her access to Jensen Huang, the head of a chip design company.

U.S. and Singaporean authorities are investigating whether Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud service provider, illegally rerouted billions of dollars’ worth of NVIDIA AI chips subject to export controls to China. The Taiwanese government has also revoked Alice Huang’s entry permit, a Hong Kong passport holder who had frequently visited Taiwan. Taiwan is investigating whether her visits were linked to intelligence gathering or violated its Anti-Infiltration Law.

According to acquaintances of the couple, Kang Wei was detained in Shanghai earlier this year on charges of stock price manipulation of listed companies but was recently released and shared a photo taken at Stanford University with friends.

Kang Wei, in his early 50s, worked as a star fund manager in China before founding the company Shanghai Hexi in 2010. Alice Huang, in her late 40s, earned a master’s degree in media management from Hong Kong Baptist University in the late 2000s.

While it is unclear when the two met, Shanghai Hexi, meaning “harmonious prosperity,” became central to their business relationship, and they began jointly managing the company in 2016.

Shanghai Hexi invested in various tech firms, including video game companies, which led Kang Wei to meet Jensen Huang, then a rising CEO in Silicon Valley, in the 2010s.

A person who helped connect the two through Kang’s business ventures told the WSJ that NVIDIA was focusing on gaming chips at the time, and Jensen Huang met Kang in Asia.

In the early 2020s, the couple shifted to cloud computing, providing remote computing power to corporate clients. They also began building relationships with major Chinese tech firms through companies in which their funds held stakes.

In 2023, the couple spotted a new opportunity: AI data centers. They acquired a Singaporean subsidiary of a video game company they had previously invested in. The subsidiary was renamed Megaspeed International, with Alice Huang listed as a director. The couple grew Megaspeed together.

The business gained momentum thanks to Alibaba, a Chinese e-commerce leader aggressively expanding into AI. After the U.S. imposed export controls on advanced chips in late 2022, Alibaba sought partners to supply computing power from NVIDIA chip-equipped data centers outside China.

Megaspeed positioned Alibaba as a key, anchor, customer to NVIDIA, earning it the status of “NVIDIA Cloud Partner” in November 2023, granting priority access to chips. Alice Huang told an acquaintance she was in contact with Jensen Huang and that Megaspeed would soon start operations smoothly.

Megaspeed transported a significant portion of its acquired NVIDIA servers to a data center in Malaysia to provide computing power to Alibaba.

Furthermore, Megaspeed shipped some NVIDIA servers to mainland China, including Century Litong, a Shanghai-based cloud computing company Kang Wei had previously dealt with. The U.S. has consistently banned exports of advanced NVIDIA AI chips to China.

From late 2024 to June 2025, investors who received investment proposals from Alice Huang and Kang Wei in Shanghai and Singapore said the couple sought funding for multiple data center projects, including two in Southeast Asia and one in Shanghai. According to these potential investors, the couple claimed they could triple investments within three years and expressed confidence in their ability to bring NVIDIA chips into China.

Alice Huang met Jensen Huang multiple times in 2024 and 2025. In May 2025, during a dinner in Taiwan, Jensen Huang’s daughter Madison Huang was photographed by Taiwanese TV network TVBS smiling and taking photos of the two with her smartphone.

During the dinner, Jensen Huang and Alice Huang briefly placed their arms around each other’s waists and shoulders, and the two even touched cheeks, with Madison Huang and her fiancé Nico Caprez present, Taiwanese media reported.
