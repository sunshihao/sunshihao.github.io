Special Envoy of the Communist Party of Cuba (PCC) and the Cuban Government, Member of the Political Bureau of the PCC Central Committee and Minister of Foreign Affairs Bruno Rodríguez Parrilla will pay an official visit to China from November 10 to 13.

At the invitation of Member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and Minister of Foreign Affairs Wang Yi, Slovakia’s Minister of Foreign and European Affairs Juraj Blanár will visit China from November 10 to 11.

Xinhua News Agency: According to reports, a Pakistani astronaut will join the Shenzhou XXIV mission crew as payload specialist and complete a short orbital flight before returning to Earth with the Shenzhou XXIII spaceship. I wonder if you have any comment on this?

Guo Jiakun: Exploration of the vast universe and peaceful use of outer space are humanity’s shared pursuit and bear on the welfare of all peoples. China advocates the peaceful use of outer space, engages in active international space cooperation, and promotes the building of a community with a shared future in the domain of outer space.

China and Pakistan are all-weather strategic cooperative partners. Having the Pakistani astronaut on board China’s spaceships will mark the first ever joint mission of Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China’s manned space program. The Pakistani astronaut will be the first foreign astronaut to step into China’s space station. This is yet another key outcome of bilateral cooperation as we mark the 75th anniversary of China-Pakistan diplomatic ties. We stand ready to work with the Pakistani side to carry forward our traditional friendship, deepen cooperation in space and other fields, and bring bilateral relations to new heights.

CCTV: Can you provide more details about the visit by Special Envoy of the PCC and Government of Cuba, Member of the Political Bureau of the PCC Central Committee and Minister of Foreign Affairs Bruno Rodríguez Parrilla?

Guo Jiakun: China and Cuba are good friends, good comrades and good brothers. The upcoming visit by Special Envoy Rodríguez speaks volumes about the high political mutual trust between our two countries. China attaches great importance to this visit. We look forward to an in-depth exchange of views on China-Cuba relations and issues of mutual interest, and will work with the Cuban side to further implement the important common understandings reached between our two leaders, strengthen communication and coordination, deepen exchanges and cooperation across the board, and continue building a China-Cuba community with a shared future.
