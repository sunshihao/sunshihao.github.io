Alice Huang, along with her husband and sister, set up a network of companies to acquire Nvidia chips, reported U.S. tech website *[The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jensen-huangs-yearslong-ties-alice-huang-enabled-nvidia-chip-sales-china).*

Alice Huang is a former executive at Singapore-based data centre company [Megaspeed](https://mothership.sg/2026/11/what-is-megaspeed/), and an alleged Chinese spy.

Her connections to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang have since come under scrutiny, after Jensen Huang [admitted to having an affair](https://mothership.sg/2026/11/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-admits-affair/).

## Met through her husband

According to the report, Jensen Huang had reportedly known Alice Huang's husband, Kang Wei, since the 2010s.

Alice was introduced to Jensen by Kang, the report claimed.

According to Chinese news outlet [*Caixin Global*](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-11-10/in-depth-woman-linked-to-nvidia-ceo-has-ties-to-chinese-fund-manager-facing-criminal-scrutiny-102503139.html), Kang is the founder of Shanghai Hexi Investment Co. Ltd., a private equity firm of which Alice was reportedly a major shareholder and senior executive.

Kang has been embroiled in controversial capital operations in recent years.

Sources claimed he was previously fined by Chinese securities regulators.

He was also recently under criminal scrutiny for manipulating listed stocks, *Caixin* reported.

However, little is known about the pair's purported relationship as of the moment.
