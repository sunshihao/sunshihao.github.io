# Nvidia shares drop after hours amid reports of meeting over CEO Jensen Huang’s future

The New York Times reported that Huang had a relationship with an Nvidia executive, following reports that he’d had a relationship with a suspected Chinese spy.

By [Rob Wile](https://www.nbcnews.com/author/rob-wile-ncpn1293609) and [Steve Kopack](https://www.nbcnews.com/author/steve-kopack-ncpn387336)

Shares of Nvidia dropped quickly in after-hours trading amid [multiple](https://www.wsj.com/business/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-30f421ad?mod=hp_twoviz_pos_1) [reports](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-board-ceo-jensen-huang-china/) that the company’s board would meet Wednesday night to discuss CEO Jensen Huang’s future and amid a New York Times report that he had a relationship with an executive who reported to him.

Shares fell as much as 8%.

[The Times report](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/11/technology/jensen-huang-nvidia-affair.html), which cited five people who worked at Nvidia, followed reports in recent days that Huang had been involved in a relationship with a woman [suspected of being a Chinese spy.](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-investigation-rcna608148)

NBC News has not confirmed the Times report or reports that the company’s board was meeting Wednesday night. [The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/business/nvidia-is-preparing-to-oust-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-30f421ad?mod=hp_twoviz_pos_1) first reported the board’s plans, citing people familiar with the matter.

Huang wrote in a note to employees Sunday that he had been “unfaithful to his wife,” though did not specify details. NBC News reported that officials believe the first woman, [Alice Huang](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/china/alice-huang-nvidia-chief-alleged-lover-suspected-chinese-spy-rcna608329), was a Chinese intelligence operative.

China has denied the allegations.
